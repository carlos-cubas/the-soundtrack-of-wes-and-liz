## 1. Title

Samira Cubas — The Soundtrack of Wes and Liz

## 2. About the game

My video game is a mini game RPG called The Soundtrack of Wes and Liz. It follows Lynn Painter’s Better Than The Movies which is told in the viewpoint of Elizabeth Buxbaum. The video game will allow Wesley Bennett to finally have his time to shine. Wes has been in love with Liz since the second grade. The objective of the game is for Wesley to win over the heart of Elizabeth. Wes will do this by completing mini games and claiming rewards throughout the levels. Libby will have the task of deciding whether Wes is her real life Mark Darcy from Pride and Prejudice. The setting of this game takes place in the year 2021 in the town of Omaha, Nebraska. The first couple puzzles take place about ten years in the past around when the two characters first met. The player must complete these challenges to determine whether they will be lovers or not. Once the first puzzles are completed, numerous other locations will be unlocked around town, including the school, the mall, Ryno’s house, and a restaurant.

## 3. Characters and plot

The Soundtrack of Wes and Liz contains the entire storyline of Better Than The Movies in Wesley’s perspective which adds an unexpected twist to the narrative. It shares hidden ideas that Libby was oblivious to for longer than she should have been. Libby likes a boy named Michael and asks Wes for his help to win him over. Wes and Liz pretend to date so that Wes can talk her up. Wes will navigate the storyline through various mini games based on key scenes in the story. You play as Wesley, the main character who has to brave his love rival, Michael, in order to win Libby’s love as the ultimate prize. Jocelyn is Libby’s best friend who doesn’t approve of Michael and has a suspicion that Liz might be falling for Wes. Helena is Libby’s stepmother who Liz doesn’t yet along with at the beginning because she is afraid of Helena trying to replace her late mother. Lastly, Liz will decide if Wes really is better than her crush since kindergarden. Each level directly relates to scene in the book where Wes has to slowly win Liz over. An important scene is where Wes scares Libby with a frog in her Barbie dreamhouse when they were younger. The next scene is where Wes scares Libby again by decapitating a gnome and putting it in the neighborhood library. About ten years in the future, Libby goes to a party and gets barfed on which gave Wes the perfect opportunity to help her out. Another important event is when Wes and Libby go the mall together to update Libby’s wardrobe. Shortly after this, Liz gets badly hit in the nose by a basketball. Soon after this, Wes and Liz go on a fake date to Libby’s favorite burger place, Stella’s. Lastly, Wesley decides to confess his feelings to Liz and silently prays she feels the same way about him.

## 4. Main Character

The player will control the character Wesley Bennett who is an eighteen year old senior in high school. Wes is tall and has an athletic build as well as dark messy hair, dark brown eyes, a defined jawline, and he usually wears a mischievous smirk on this face. He is a very talented baseball player and he plans on pursuing it as a career in the near future. Wesley has many redeeming qualities and strengths including his ability to be trustworthy, empathetic, remorseful, kind, and loyal. Bennett’s fatal flaw is that he is deathly afraid of clowns. Wesley has a baseball cap, baseball bat, boom box, and his number 32 jersey in his inventory that he can equip only once. The baseball cap gives the player hints, the baseball bat allows the player to skip an obstacle, his boombox makes him invincible for thirty seconds, and his number 32 jersey gives him another life. Wesley’s favorite pair of shoes that he bought for Libby once are Chuck Taylors. The player must find the hidden pair somewhere in the game in order to maneuver at double times their original speed.

## 5. NPCs

The video game has four NPCs that support the structure of the game and the plot of the book itself. Michael has been Libby’s dreamboat since childhood which means Wes has some serious competition. Wesley’s love rival appears in the mini games as well as in the narrations that come between each level. These narrations give the player a basic understanding of the story line. Another character that will assist with the story’s progression is Helena. Libby’s mother passed away in her childhood so Liz wasn’t too particularly keen on having a new mother figure. Once Libby recognized that Helena was only trying to be Libby’s friend instead of replacing her late mother, they got along just fine. Helena will be the a character that the shows up in some of the narrations. Helena personally thinks Wes should become Libby’s other half. Jocelyn is Libby’s best friend who doesn’t think Michael is the one for Liz and instead believes that Libby’s true love should be Wesley. Libby doesn’t tell Jocelyn that she and Wes are fake dating because she doesn’t think Joss will approve of her plan for Michael to become her lover. Wesley will have to get Jocelyn’s blessing in order to continue on in his pursuit of Libby’s heart. To achieve this, he will have to pass one of the game’s hardest levels that will be conducted by, none other than, Jocelyn. The final NPC that will be featured in The Soundtracks of Wes and Liz is Elizabeth Buxbaum herself. Liz spent most of her childhood watching rom-coms, so this will be her moment to decide whether Wesley Bennett is her Mark Darcy from Pride and Prejudice.

## 6. Level 1 & 2

**Level 1: Barbie and the Frog.** Narration: I have loved Libby since the second grade when I realized that I could turn her cheeks pink with just a word. We had just finished watching The Princess and the frog in school when I came to the conclusion that Liz would love a frog in her Barbie dream house. I didn’t consider that she might produce a sense of disgust toward the little creatures -Wesley Bennett. Mini game: The first level is based off of the original Donkey Kong game, The Princess & the frog, and the first chapter of the book. The mini game takes place in Libby’s Barbie Dreamhouse where Barbie will throw beachballs down at a frog. The player will control the frog in this level and will face the challenge of dodging and jumping over the incoming beachballs. The objective of the game is for the frog to make it to the roof where he will get a kiss from Barbie. Once the level is completed, the player will receive Public service announcement (interlude) by Jay-Z as the first addition to the soundtrack.

**Level 2: The Great Gnome Decapitation.** Narration: I tried to make up for the frog incident by gifting Libby a guardian gnome for her little neighborhood library. As you could imagine, giving a little girl a severed gnome head might not have been the best way to go. -Wesley Bennett. Mini game: The second level of the Soundtrack of Wes and Liz is based off chapter 1 and the carnival game Whack-a-mole. The player will control Wesley who will have the task of grabbing gnomes that are popping out of the ground. This will challenge the player because every time the player collects a gnome, the speed of the gnomes popping in and out of the ground will increase. The objective of this mini game is for the player to collect ten gnomes in order to surprise Libby when she opens her little library. If the player succeeds, Enter Sandman by Metallica will be rewarded to them.

## 12. Side Quests

The Soundtrack of Wes and Liz will have three different side quests that will help the player get ahead on winning over Liz. Liz has spent a lot of time and energy changing herself for her childhood crush instead of accepting herself. Wes will make it known that she is perfect just the way she is and that if Michael can’t see this, it’s his loss. Wes should teach Liz that she needs to accept herself instead of breaking her back trying to be what others want from her. Wes will teach her this by taking her to dinner with by making sure its not the Hollywood perfect version she was expecting. Liz will notice that Wes still treats her the same way he did before, meaning she doesn’t have to change herself for anyone. This will gain Wes some trust and respect from Liz and will help during her final decision. Liz and Helena don’t get along easily because Libby is afraid that if she becomes close with Helena, She will forget about her late mother. Wesley will try to show Liz that the only way for her to grow is by accepting that she cannot hold tightly onto past memories in order to avoid the pain of the present. If the player is able to teach her this by going through a series of puzzles, Wes will convince Liz that he is reliable and will always be there for her which will also help her make the decision. Wes sees that Libby’s car door window is open during a thunderstorm. He decides to rush out during the storm to close the window because he knows that she has to drive to work early the next morning. Liz will see Wes drenched to the bone in the front yard and will ask him what he was doing. Wes and Liz have an enemies bit going so he says ”I just didn't want your moldy car ruining the property value of the whole neighborhood, Buxbaum.” This way Libby will think about what he said to her all night. If the player does all of these steps, they will find a hidden pair of Chuck Taylors in the back of Wesley’s car from Liz.

Controller (not adapted literally — this is an iPhone game): four buttons move back, forward, left and right. The blue button is what the player should press when they are rewarded with an item at the end of the level to claim it. The red button allows the player to play the song that they added to the soundtrack. The orange button can be used if the player wants to hear the narration at the beginning of the level. The green button allows the player to click onto different locations on the world map as well as interact with the mini games. The left trigger allows the player to use whatever they have equipped from their inventory. The right trigger equips items that are in the players inventory.

## 15. Theme

Theme main theme in Better than the Movies by Lynn Painter is that true love is built of communication, mutual trust, and vulnerability. Liz has always waited for grand romantic gestures from her childhood crush, Michael. What Libby never noticed were the small but significant ones that Wes always conveyed toward her. This connects to The Soundtrack of Wes and Liz because Wes has to go through numerous different challenges to show Liz something that would surely change her perspective. Liz had to realize that it’s not the grand gestures that matter, it’s the small but expressive indications that really build a strong relationship. This is how Libby learned that true love isn’t like those rom-coms she grew up watching, true love is built on respect, trust, empathy, and long-term commitment. During the boss level of the game, Wes creates this big romantic gesture in order to catch Libby’s attention. When she hears the confession rap, she realizes that Wes truly loves her because of the small, significant moments that they shared together. This is when Liz realizes that Wes is her Mark Darcy from Pride and Prejudice, not Michael. Other themes are that self acceptance is important and that holding onto memories of the past will not help save you from any pain in the present. The side quests of the game explain these themes in detail so that the player truly understands the message.

## 16. Reflection

The hardest part about adapting my novel into a video game was that the quiet scenes couldn’t be added or else the player wouldn’t have any mini games to play. In the original story, Liz was the one who started the confession instead of Wes. Although Wes does join in and finish it off, Liz was the one who explained how she felt about him first. I changed this in the video game because Wes was supposed to be the one who goes through all this effort in order to show Liz that he is worthy of her love. Lynn Painter wanted readers to understand that real-life human connections are messier, more authentic, and better than how the movies try to convey love. The video game preserves this message by making it known that Liz was always waiting for the perfect Hollywood-type romance until she herself realized, with the help of Wesley, that true love isn’t perfect and it is messy but it’s also much better than what the movies promote it to be. If Lynn Painter played The Soundtrack of Wes and Liz, I think she would appreciate it because it keeps all of the themes and most of the storyline intact. I think she would also find it interesting that I switched it into Wesley’s perspective to give players insight on how Wesley may have felt during the events of the book.
